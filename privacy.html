Privacy policy

EveryDime for iPhone. Last updated November 3, 2026.

EveryDime is a personal money diary. This page explains what it stores, what leaves your phone and why, and how to remove it. In short: your data stays on your iPhone and in your own Google account. EveryDime has no server, no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and never sells or shares your data.

What's stored on your iPhone

The payments, income, refunds, categories, limits, people you share costs with, cards and receipts you add are stored in the app's private storage on your iPhone, protected by iOS. Receipt photos and PDFs are kept there too. Secrets such as sign-in tokens and API keys are kept in the iPhone Keychain.

What leaves your phone, and only if you turn it on

  • Reading receipts with AI. If you add your own Google Gemini API key, the text read from a receipt, an e-receipt or something you said is sent to Google's Gemini API to fill in the details. Card numbers are removed first, and photos themselves aren't sent. Google's Gemini API terms apply to that text, and on its free tier Google may use it to improve its products. Without a key, receipts are read on your phone only.
  • Voice entries. What you say is turned into text by Apple's speech recognition, which may use Apple's servers.
  • Google Drive backup. If you sign in with Google, EveryDime backs up your data and receipts once a day to an EveryDime folder in your own Google Drive, and can restore them on a new phone.

All of this goes directly between your iPhone and Google or Apple. It never passes through anyone else.

Google user data

When you sign in with Google, EveryDime asks for two things:

  • Your email address, only to show which account is signed in.
  • Access to the files EveryDime creates in your Google Drive (the drive.file permission). It uses this to save, list, download and remove its own backups and receipts in the EveryDime folder. It can't see or change any other file in your Drive.

This data is used only to provide backup and restore for you. It isn't used for advertising, isn't sold, isn't shared with anyone, and isn't used to train AI models. No person reads it.

EveryDime's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

  • Deleting the app deletes everything stored on your iPhone.
  • Signing out in Settings, Backup removes EveryDime's access to your Google account. You can also remove it at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • Your Drive backups and receipts are ordinary files in your Drive. Delete the EveryDime folder to remove them.

Children

EveryDime isn't meant for children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their data.

Changes
