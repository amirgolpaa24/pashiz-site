Privacy policy

Pashiz for iPhone, first called EveryDime. Last updated November 10, 2026.

Pashiz is a personal money diary. This page explains what it stores, what leaves your phone and why, and how to remove it. In short: your data stays on your iPhone, and in your own Google account if you choose to back up there. Pashiz has no server, no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and never sells or shares your data.

What's stored on your iPhone

The payments, income, refunds, categories, limits, people you share costs with, cards and receipts you add are stored in the app's private storage on your iPhone, protected by iOS. Receipt photos and PDFs are kept there too. Secrets such as sign-in tokens and API keys are kept in the iPhone Keychain.

What leaves your phone, and only if you turn it on

  • Reading receipts with AI. If you add your own Google Gemini API key, the text read from a receipt or an e-receipt, or something you said or typed, is sent to Google's Gemini API to fill in the details. Card numbers are removed first, and photos themselves aren't sent. Google's Gemini API terms apply to that text, and on its free tier Google may use it to improve its products. Without a key, all of these are read on your phone only.
  • Voice entries. What you say is turned into text by Apple's speech recognition, which may use Apple's servers.
  • Google Drive. If you choose Google Drive for backups, Pashiz keeps a Pashiz folder in your own Google Drive in step with your phone: each change within seconds, a full copy once a day, and your receipts, so a new phone can get exactly the same data. Your Gemini API key, if you added one, is kept there too, in a separate settings file, so a new phone has it after you sign in. Pashiz also checks how much of your Google storage is used, so it can warn you before it fills up. If you choose This iPhone only, nothing is backed up anywhere.
  • Bank alert emails. If you turn them on, Pashiz looks in your Gmail for emails from banks and card companies, reads the ones about a payment, and adds each payment to your Inbox in the app. It does this on your iPhone, when you open Pashiz and now and then in the background, and never sends these emails to Gemini or any other AI, even with AI reading on. Only the payment it finds is kept, not the email.

All of this goes directly between your iPhone and Google or Apple. It never passes through anyone else.

Google user data

When you sign in with Google, Pashiz asks for:

  • Your email address, only to show which account is signed in.
  • Access to the files Pashiz creates in your Google Drive (the drive.file permission). It uses this to save, list, download, rename and remove its own copies, changes, settings and receipts in the Pashiz folder, and to see how much of your Google storage is used. It can't see or change any other file in your Drive.
  • Read-only access to your Gmail (the gmail.readonly permission), only if you turn on bank alert emails. Pashiz uses it only to search for emails from banks and card companies and to read the ones about a payment. It never sends, changes or deletes email.

This data is used only to back up and restore your data, and to turn your bank's alerts into entries for you to check. It isn't used for advertising, isn't sold, isn't shared with anyone, and isn't used to train AI models. No person reads it. Emails from your Gmail are read on your iPhone and never sent to an AI model.

Pashiz's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

  • Erase everything, in Settings, removes all of Pashiz's data from your iPhone and deletes the Pashiz folder from your Drive.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything stored on your iPhone.
  • Turning off bank alert emails, in Settings, stops Pashiz reading your Gmail.
  • Signing out of your Google account, at the top of Settings, removes Pashiz's access to it. You can also remove it at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • Your Drive backups and receipts are ordinary files in your Drive. Delete the Pashiz folder to remove them.

Children

Pashiz isn't meant for children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their data.
