EveryDime

A personal money diary for iPhone.

EveryDime keeps track of what you spend and earn, in seconds. Scan a receipt, share an e-receipt, say what you bought, or type it in, and it fills in the store, the amount, the items and a category for you to check.

  • Categories with monthly limits, so you can see what's left to spend.
  • Shared costs: who paid, who owes what, split evenly, by amounts or by item.
  • Refunds that come off the purchase they belong to.
  • Each item's real cost, with tax, fees and discounts shared out.
  • A daily backup of everything, with your receipts, to your own Google Drive.

Your data stays yours. Everything is stored on your iPhone. There's no EveryDime account and no EveryDime server. If you turn on Google Drive backup, the backups go to your own Drive, and EveryDime can only see the files it creates there.

EveryDime is a personal project and isn't on the App Store.
