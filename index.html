Pashiz

A personal money diary for iPhone. Pashiz (پشیز) is the Persian name for the smallest coin.

Pashiz keeps track of what you spend and earn, in seconds. Scan a receipt, share an e-receipt, say what you bought, or type it in, and it fills in the store, the amount, the items and a category for you to check.

  • Categories with monthly limits, so you can see what's left to spend.
  • Shared costs: who paid, who owes what, split evenly, by amounts or by item.
  • Refunds that come off the purchase they belong to.
  • Each item's real cost, with tax, fees and discounts shared out.
  • Everything kept in step with your own Google Drive, receipts included, so a new phone gets exactly the same data.

Your data stays yours. Everything is stored on your iPhone. There's no Pashiz account and no Pashiz server. If you turn on Google Drive, your data goes to your own Drive, and Pashiz can only see the files it creates there.

Pashiz is a personal project and isn't on the App Store. It was first called EveryDime.
